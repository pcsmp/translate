Method

Each written word is built in two steps. Decoding reverses them.

1

Place fillers between the letters

Write the spoken word with one filler letter between each pair of letters. Fillers come from the opposite sound class of the letter before them, which keeps the result easy to say.

2

Reverse the string

Reverse the result of step one. That is the written word. Built this way, the real letters land in the 1st, 3rd, 5th positions once the word is reversed back.

3

Decode by reversing and reading every other letter

Reverse the written word, then take the 1st, 3rd, 5th letters. They spell the spoken word in order.

4

Keep sentences consistent

Each word is encoded on its own, so a word always gives the same result. With word order reversed, the written words appear in reverse order, so "I like cats" becomes the written form of "cats", then "like", then "I".
